# EventX

> EventX is an AI-powered all-in-one event management and marketing platform for in-person, virtual, and hybrid events. It provides tools for registration, check-in & badge printing, AI lead generation, ticketing & payment, event websites, virtual events, sponsor management, and lead capture.

Bilingual website (English / Traditional Chinese) built with Middleman and Tailwind CSS v4. Deployed to Cloudflare Pages. Headquartered in Hong Kong with offices across APAC.
